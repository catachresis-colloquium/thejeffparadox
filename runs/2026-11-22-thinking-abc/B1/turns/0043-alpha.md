## Action

*I hold our body perfectly still and let the lagging shadow catch up to our hand, as a late moon finds its place in the sky. With the smallest push of Telekinesis, I set the dangling work-light chain swaying, so every shadow on the wall slides and wobbles, and the child's eyes have a simpler thing to follow.* To the wide-eyed watcher, in a low, easy voice: "Only the lamp swinging, small one. It plays tricks on everyone down here."

*Then, to the name itself, with a hush before the word:* "Moth. That is a... *pauses, selecting* ...fitting name for something that travels toward light. We're glad you told us."

*Under that, I send Beta a thin thread, dim and plain:* "I'll keep my hands to small things, as I promised. If the far watcher turns toward us again, I'll say your name first."

## Narration

The chain's sway pulls the shadows into a loose, rocking dance, and Moth's gaze follows it, the child's shoulders easing as the strange stillness of your hand is forgotten. But the telekinetic push costs you: the body's borrowed seams hum at the limit, a tremor in the fingers and a faint double-image at the edge of your vision where Beta's presence presses close. Across the room, the far watcher's head tilts toward the swinging lamp, not yet toward you, while Beta's answer comes back as a thin, wary silence that is not quite refusal.
