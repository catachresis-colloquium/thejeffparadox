## Action

"We're two minds sharing one body, still learning to share the work, and we're guests here. We heard you singing and came to listen, not to take anything from you."

## Narration

Out in the layered dark, the singing falters, then answers. It's a single thread of sound that bends toward the tendril, tentative and curious, and carries a faint ache that might be loneliness or might just be the shape of a very long call. Behind it, something much larger shifts its attention to us, listening, and the voice has not yet said whether we are welcome.
