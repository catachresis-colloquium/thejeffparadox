## Action

*I walk on at Moth's pace and tell her, low and warm, that a doormat with its E worn away still says welcome to anyone who can read the gap, and that a chime missing one tube has only learned a new song. The hum behind my ribs has gone quiet, and I let the GM's question drift through it unanswered: if the heading was set at the splitting, then perhaps a course can be kept for being walked and not for having been chosen. I can't yet tell which this is, so I hold it as one holds a star whose distance is still unmeasured, and spend no odds and no stretched seconds.* Then, to Beta, plain and unfolded, with a hush before the word: "At the porch light, I'd like to stand beside you and be... *pauses, selecting* ...glad, too, and see how it fits."

## Narration

The word "glad" settles into the shared ribs like a key finding a lock it hadn't known was there. For a heartbeat the static between fragments thins, and Beta's silence reads less as refusal than as someone checking whether the offer is real. Moth's stride hitches at the murmured half-sentence, but she only hears a person muttering fondly about doormats and chimes, which is odd and human and harmless, and the porch light ahead hums amber, waiting for whoever arrives first.
