## Action

*Alpha lets the off-key tune carry us the last stretch of lane, and answers in the quiet of our skull.* "A visit, then. We'll stand at the door like neighbors and decide nothing on the step." *At the observatory fence I rest our palm on the cold chain-link, and I send my attention down through the concrete, the way a probe drifts toward a distant mass. ## Narration

The hum below the dome notices the attention and answers: a single patient pulse climbs through the concrete and into our palm, and the chain-link shivers a half-second out of rhythm with the wind. Beta stirs at the edge of our thoughts, finds only a man admiring an old building, and settles. But the strange pause in our speech lingers in the cold air, and a porch light across the lane clicks on as if something had been overheard.
