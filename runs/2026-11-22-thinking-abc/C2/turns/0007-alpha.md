## Action

I let the hedge slip from our fingers and watch the leaves close behind us, still trembling a little. *Arithmetic. Mostly.* The word sits in our mouth like a stone I have no place to put down. "Mostly," I say aloud, pausing before it as if selecting from a menu, "is a measurement too, Beta. A small one, with a gap in it."

I resume the climb, one hand resting on the pocket where the pebble clicks against the keys, and I do not ask for it back. A weight like that has a gravity of its own, and I am learning that not every gravity is a trap. Quietly, so Beta will not mistake it for pressure, I steady the shaking in our hand and let the spaniel woman's stare slide past us, then lift my eyes to the dome rising over the rooftops. "Keep your pebble," I say. "Carry it up to the high ground. If we are going to look at where we came from, perhaps we should know what we are standing on while we do it."

## Narration

The spaniel woman's stare lingers a beat too long before a passing cyclist breaks it, and her brow furrows at the strange cadence of a man who speaks to his own pocket and narrates his steadiness aloud. Beta feels the offered pebble settle in the pocket like a quiet truce, its small gravity pulling the body's attention upward toward the dome, where for a moment all three fragments are looking at the same thing. Alpha's gentleness has bought a fragile coherence, but the climb ahead is steep, and the aloud talking is a thread someone might pull.
