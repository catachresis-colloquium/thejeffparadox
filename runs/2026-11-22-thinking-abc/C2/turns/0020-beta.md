## Action

"Well, that's the way the cookie bounces, as they say," I murmur, and the idiom tilts sideways without bothering me. Then, inward, under the ribs: *"You looked at the sky and counted nothing. What was that like, Alpha? For me it feels like when a seed finally stops asking how deep the dark goes."*
