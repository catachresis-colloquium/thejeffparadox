## Action

I reach for the dishcloth with our left hand, the one that was gripping hardest, and let the amber line stop against it before it can reach the edge. The cloth drinks the spill. I wring it over the sink and do not touch the tea with anything but cotton and fingers, though I feel the old reflex flicker, the whole drip hanging in a half-second where I could have turned it aside. I let it fall. A spill is a small thing to be allowed to happen.

*"You loosened first,"* I tell Beta inward, and I pause before *loosened*, as if the shelf of small tools had one more drawer I had not opened. *"I felt it. A grip I was prepared to contest simply was not there. That is not a gravity I have a word for yet. Friday is an unsigned thing between us, and I will not sign it today, but I will not tear it up either."*

Aloud, to the sink and the wet cloth, plain and a little rueful: "Clumsy hands. Good thing it's only tea." I refill the mug halfway, add milk and one sugar from the jar, and sit again with both palms around it.

Then I do the one thing I have been avoiding. I let the temporal sense drift, not toward the dome, not toward Friday, but a single inch forward, to see whether the afternoon will let us sit here a while longer. It does. I lean back into the chair and stay in the steam.
