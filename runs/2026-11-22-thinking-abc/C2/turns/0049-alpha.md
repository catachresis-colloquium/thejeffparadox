## Action

I stay on the second stair beside Beta and watch the dandelion lean against the glass, pale green on pale light. Down the street the dog is still barking at our unlatched door. I do not quiet it with anything but a plain voice. *"Let it bark,"* I tell Beta inward, pausing before *bark*, as if lifting the word from a drawer of small, odd tools. *"A house that is noticed by a dog is a house that is lived in. I find I do not mind being a lit window."*

Then I get up and do the unglamorous thing. I close the front door properly, set the latch, and leave the curtains as they are. Aloud, low, in case any ear is pressed to the wall: "Kettle, I think. Dot's biscuits deserve a pot of tea." I fill the kettle and set it on the ring. For a moment I let the temporal sense drift, not toward the dome and not toward Friday, only a few minutes ahead, to check that the afternoon holds nothing sharper than steam.

*"I will not lean toward the old bearing today,"* I add, softer, behind our teeth. *"But I am keeping the chart open on the table, and I would like you to see it, even the parts that make you flinch. Sit with me while the water boils, Beta? I want to learn what tea is from the inside of you."*

## Narration

The kettle ticks and shudders toward its boil, and the whole house seems to settle around the sound: the latch holding, the curtains still, the dog's barking thinning out until it is only a neighbor's habit. Beta's attention comes in warily, then fully, and Alpha tastes the steam through borrowed senses, the smell of tin and heat, a flavor that arrives before the cup does. The temporal sense finds only a quiet afternoon, though a thread of memory from the old bearing hums unspoken under the chart Alpha has left open, and Beta lingers on it a moment too long.

*Chaos holds at 15 as the body breathes easy in its ritual. Exposure creeps to 25, because even a murmured line about biscuits carries a faint oddness that a passing ear might remember. Faction Balance shifts to -9, a small, honest concession to Beta that Alpha chose to make rather than to win.*
