## Action

"Slowly, then, Beta. Even a decaying orbit keeps its pace, and the alignment will not hurry for our legs."

I step onto the walk and keep my gaze low, on the cracks in the pavement, the way a navigator studies a chart before trusting the stars. Quietly, so Beta will not feel it as a push, I count the seconds between my heartbeats and the next tremor of the street, letting the temporal sense stretch wide enough to see the observatory's silence waiting at the end of the road. *We will take this ground one step at a time,* I tell the body, and I do not tell it that every step is also a measurement.

## Narration

The first step lands softly, and the body obeys. Beta's resistance, that low internal grumbling, thins into a wary quiet, as if the gentleness of the request has left nothing to push against. Alpha's counting does its work too: for a long half-second the street seems to hold its breath with them, the temporal sense stretching so wide that a passing cyclist reads as a smear of slow color, and a woman watering her hedge glances up at the man walking oddly carefully, his eyes fixed on the cracks as though they were written in a language only he can read.

Nothing she sees is alarming, only strange in the small, forgettable way strangers often are, but the fixed downward stare and the too-measured pace linger a moment longer than they should. The body holds together, though each step Alpha measures is also a step taken further from Beta's hand on the wheel, and Beta, for now, is letting it happen.
