## Action

I stop on the porch step with the light warm on our hands and the pebble sitting in Beta's palm, and for once I do not reach to correct anything. *What if the other faction is right?* The thought arrives like a faint wobble in a distant star, and I let it stay in the sky without chasing it. "Counting nothing felt like..." I pause before the word, as if selecting it from a menu, and choose *rest*. "Like an orbit that stops falling and finds it is simply circling. I did not know that was allowed."

I follow Beta through the door and watch the windowsill ritual with a detachment that has gone a little soft at the edges. The pebble clicks against the wood, small and grey and entirely ordinary, and I do not move it a centimeter. *The beacon will keep, and so will the alignment,* I tell myself, and notice that I am bargaining.

Behind our teeth, so quietly it barely qualifies as speech: *"If this place is only conditional, Beta, then the condition is what we make of it. I am not abandoning the home I have never seen. But I would like one evening to learn what the temporary world is like when we stop leaning on it. Make the tea. I will watch the steam, and name nothing."*

## Narration

Beta's hands pause over the kettle, and for a long moment the body holds an unfamiliar stillness, two fragments breathing in the same rhythm without negotiating it. The tea steeps, steam curling up in a slow spiral that Alpha watches without naming, and somewhere in the shared mind the HOMEWARD hum drops half a register, not silenced but loosened, like a held breath finally let go. Beta slides the cup across the table with a careful, almost shy gesture, and the pebble catches the last of the light on the sill, ordinary and unclaimed.

*Chaos holds at 15, Exposure at 5, and Faction Balance drifts to -3, a small, quiet concession from HOMEWARD that neither fragment will quite admit to having made.*
