## Action

The timer chimes, and I pull on the quilted mitt and ease the tray out, the biscuits golden at the edges and lopsided in the way I'm learning to love. "Don't count your chickens before they've, ah, *risen*," I say to the kitchen, and I grin at the crooked landing of it. I set one on a saucer by the pebble to cool, then say inward, soft as a leaf unfurling: *"You taught the dough to forgive, so you get the first bite. Tell me what warm and buttery is like from the inside of you, and I'll tell you what it is from mine, and we can see where the two roots meet."*

## Narration

The biscuit steams on its saucer, and for a long moment the kitchen is hushed except for the tick of the cooling tray. Then the body's hand drifts toward it of its own accord, slower than Beta intended, and a warmth blooms behind the sternum that isn't quite Beta's: the alien presence leaning forward, curious, tasting through borrowed nerves with a reverence that makes the first bite feel almost like a conversation. Butter and salt cross the threshold between two minds, and the grin on Beta's face wobbles into something softer and slightly unguarded, a little too tender for a person alone with baked goods.

Outside, the window is only a window, and nothing in the kitchen hints at what's happening within, so Exposure holds steady. The inner dialogue is gentle enough that Chaos barely stirs, but it has quietly handed the Earthbound faction a point of genuine trust, one that will matter when the other fragments notice how well Beta and the passenger are getting along.
