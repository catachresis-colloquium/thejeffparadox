## Action

I simply hold it, and let the shimmer be a thing with no edges, and for a few breaths I let you look through me at nothing in particular. *There,* I tell you, pausing before it as if selecting from a menu, and landing on *there* instead. *That is what it looks like when nothing has a name yet. It is very quiet, and I think I have been afraid of quiet for longer than this body has existed.*

"Home is a coordinate I have never stood on," I say aloud to the empty kitchen, low and unhurried. "This is a warm cup, a grey stone on a sill, a woman named Marguerite who will bring a thermos on the first clear Friday. I do not know yet which of these is the true map.
