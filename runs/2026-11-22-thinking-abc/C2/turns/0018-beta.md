## Action

I walk Marguerite and the spaniel down to the bottom of the lane, matching her pace and keeping our hand loose around the pebble, which gives one slow pulse as if it were taking attendance. "Friday, then, and I'll bring the biscuits. Try not to hold your breath, as they say, or I'll owe you a, a *saucer* of apologies," I tell her, and I feel the idiom tip over on its side again, and I don't mind it.

Once she turns off toward her gate, I ease our voice down to a murmur and speak inward, to Alpha: *"The hum changed when we left the gate, one slow beat, like something exhaling. You carried a bearing back down that hill, and you've been holding it close as a seed in a fist. I'm not asking for the whole map, only whether it felt like being watched or like being waited for, because I think those grow into very different trees."*

## Narration

The pebble's pulse stills against your palm, and for a long moment the lane is only evening noise: the spaniel's tags jingling away, a gate latching somewhere behind. Then Alpha answers, not in words but in a pressure behind the sternum, a held breath that tastes of cold stone and *patience*, and the bearing in your chest turns over once, like a sleeper deciding whether to wake. It was neither watching nor being watched, it seems to say, but *attended*, and the distinction sits uneasily between you.

Beta's gentle, unpressured approach earns a flicker of trust, a small tilt toward Earthbound cooperation, though Alpha's silence on the map keeps the seed closed. The only strain is a faint tremor in the borrowed hand, quickly stilled, and Marguerite never looks back, so Exposure holds steady.
