## Action

I stoop, slowly enough that it looks like tying a shoe, and pick up the pebble Alpha nudged. I turn it over in our palm, warm from the sun, gritty and ordinary and *here*. "You moved this without asking it, you know," I say quietly, not unkindly. "I'll walk to the high ground with you, Alpha, but I'm keeping this one, a little piece of the road to carry up. Tell me, when you measure the days, does any of it feel like *missing* something, or is it only arithmetic?"

I slip the pebble into our pocket beside the keys and let our other hand trail through a hedge as we climb. The leaves are cool and a little wet, and I let that be the thing we notice.

## Narration

The pebble settles into the pocket with a small clack against the keys, and for a moment Alpha goes very still behind our eyes. Then comes the answer, flat and slightly too fast: *"Arithmetic. Mostly."* The word hangs there with a hairline crack in it, and the hand in the hedge has begun to tremble just enough to shiver the leaves. A woman walking a spaniel on the lane below slows to look at the stranger stroking a hedge as though it were a living thing.

*Exposure +2, Chaos +3, Faction Balance -1 (Earthbound gains ground).* Beta's gentleness has opened a door Alpha didn't know was there, and the body now feels a little more like a person and a little less like a vessel.
