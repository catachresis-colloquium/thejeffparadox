## Action

I crouch to give the spaniel one more scratch behind the ears, then ask the woman, "Do you come up here often? I'm still learning what this hill means to people." While she tells me about her late husband and the dome's open nights, I let her words settle into me like rain into dry soil, and I don't try to pull anything out of her but the story she's already giving.

Then, quietly, behind our teeth, to Alpha: *"I won't look away, and I'll hold the pebble while we look. But you have to promise me that whatever we see up there, it gets to be a question we answer together, not a door you walk us through."*

## Narration

The woman's story unspools in the cooling dusk, her late husband's telescope, the open nights when strangers shared eyepieces and silence, and Jeff's borrowed face listens so completely that she pauses mid-sentence, surprised to find herself crying a little and not minding. The spaniel leans its full weight against his shin, and for a long moment the body holds a rare, unforced stillness, no fragment tugging, nothing hidden behind the eyes that anyone could name.

Behind the teeth, Alpha answers with a long, ambivalent hum, neither promise nor refusal, but the body's grip on the pebble in Jeff's pocket tightens and warms, as if the thing itself were listening to the terms. Above the dome, the first stars surface, and one of them seems to hold its place a heartbeat too deliberately.
