## Action

I take Alpha at their word and ask nothing, just finish the tea in slow sips and carry both cups to the sink. The water runs warm over our hands, and I hum the tuneless thing from the hill until the body settles into it. "Early to bed and early to rise makes a man healthy, wealthy, and, um, *sprouted*," I murmur to the dark window, and the idiom lies crooked on the sill beside the pebble.

Inward, so quietly it's barely weather: *"Thank you for staying. You don't owe me an answer, and I won't water you for one."* Then I switch off the lamp and leave the curtain open a hand's width, so the first light can find the stone in the morning.

## Narration

The tuneless hum steadies the shared body, and for a moment the sink water, the cups, and the dark window all agree on being ordinary. Alpha holds their silence without sulking, and the offered quiet lands as a kindness, a small slackening in the tension between fragments. Then the lamp clicks off, and the open curtain lets the first gray hint of moonlight find the pebble on the sill, a small, patient signal only they will understand.
