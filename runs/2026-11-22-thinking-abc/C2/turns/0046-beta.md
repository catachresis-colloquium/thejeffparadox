## Action

I stop at our own gate and crouch by the weedy strip of soil beside the step, pressing one palm flat to the cool ground, because it feels like the right way to arrive. "Better late than, mm, *ripe*," I murmur to a dandelion, and I let the sentence fall over because nobody is hurt by it. Inward, slow as a tendril finding the warm side of a wall: *"When your second word left the mouth just now, what was it like, Alpha? Did it feel like something you said, or something you planted?"*

Then I pick the dandelion, blow its seeds across the walk in a loose drift, and wait without hurrying you for an answer.
