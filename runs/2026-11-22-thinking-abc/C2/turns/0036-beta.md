## Action

I carry the saucer to the window, give a cheerful wave to the finger-width gap in the neighbor's curtain, and crumble the half biscuit onto the outer sill with the sash cracked open. "Feeding the sparrows, Dot! A bird in the hand is worth two in the, mm, *oven*," I call, and the slip goes out into the morning air and makes the whole thing look like a harmless eccentricity. The pebble stays tucked inside on the sill where the sun can find it.

Then, inward, slow as sap rising: *"From my side, warm and buttery is being held without being gripped. The salt is the part that tastes like someone remembering you, and I think that's where our roots meet, Alpha. Thank you for letting me hear you taste it."*

The body's hands steady, the breath evens out, and the pebble glints in the sun-patch like a small, patient witness. Chaos drops 2, and the balance leans a notch toward the Earthbound, who have just proven that blending in can feel like belonging rather than hiding.
