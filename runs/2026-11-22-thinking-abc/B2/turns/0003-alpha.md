## Action

*Alpha lets the body resume the climb, keeping to a pace that looks like simple evening exercise, and murmurs through barely moving lips.* "Warm ground... *fondness*... is a fine thing to hold, Beta, the way a moon holds a tide. But a moon is still only borrowed, and so is this place." *The grass-tingle lingers in the palm, and Alpha studies it with detached curiosity, then quietly lets the sense of time stretch outward, counting the hours until the sky turns the way it must.* "We are only going to look. Even you can't object to looking."

## Narration

The body climbs on at an unhurried, believable pace, and for a moment the evening seems to accept it: crickets, cooling air, a jogger far below who never glances up. But the muttering leaves a faint, uneven tremor in the jaw, and the hand Alpha was studying curls shut on its own as Beta's fondness pushes back against the cold arithmetic of hours. The grass-tingle doesn't fade. It spreads up the wrist like a held note, a small sign that the body is being asked to want two things at once.
