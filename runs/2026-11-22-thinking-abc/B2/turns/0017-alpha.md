## Action

*Alpha keeps the borrowed hands slack and the posture unchanged, and thins the time-sense just enough to read the stranger's breathing against the crickets, slow and unguarded, like a steady star. Then, with a little of the body's voice and none of its urgency, Alpha tips the face toward the dark dome.* "Quiet hill, isn't it? Does anyone still open that dome for the sky, or has it been left to keep its own company?" *Beneath the casual words, Alpha murmurs to Beta without moving the lips:* "One small question, Beta... *courtesy*... the way one asks a neighbor the hour. If it troubles you, take the near side back, and we will let the answer fall wherever it falls."

## Narration

The stranger's breathing hitches, then settles, and he chuckles at the dome as if the question were the most natural thing on the hill. "Old observatory? County shut it years back. Kids still hop the fence sometimes, but nobody's opened the shutters in ages." Inside, Beta's resistance loosens a little at the courtesy, a held breath let go, though the offer of the near side stays unclaimed and hums between them. The body stays steady, its voice easy and its posture unremarkable, and the only oddity is that the face lingers on the dome a heartbeat longer than a human's would.

**Effects:** Chaos −1 (24), since the calm, consensual tone steadies the body. Exposure +1 (6), for the slightly-too-long stare and the unusually careful phrasing. Faction balance shifts slightly toward Homeward, since Alpha now has a lead on the dome and Beta hasn't contested it.
