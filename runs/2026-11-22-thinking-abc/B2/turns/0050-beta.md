## Action

*Beta slows at a low garden wall and perches on it a moment, the way a tired man might, and lets the pale hill go on being just a hill between the chimneys. The leaf in the pocket gets one more press of the thumb, then is left in peace.* "No thanks needed, Alpha. Anybody can chat up a privet, but it takes grit to stand still in front of something you haven't got a name for."

*In the dark we share, Beta keeps its voice low and rooted, like a seedling that has just found water.* "Here's one more I can't look up. When you noticed the hill and didn't stop to measure it, did that feel like holding something back, or more like finally setting a heavy bag down?"

## Narration

The body settles onto the garden wall with a human sag in the shoulders, and for a few breaths nothing about it is remarkable: a tired man in the dusk, one hand in a pocket, a streetlamp flickering on behind him. Inside, Beta's question lands in the shared dark and the usual static of competing impulses dips to a hush. The borrowed pulse steadies, and the memory of the unmeasured hill lingers like warmth in a stone, a small point for the Earthbound way of simply being somewhere. A passing neighbor's dog sniffs at the wall, wags once, and moves on, having found nothing strange.
