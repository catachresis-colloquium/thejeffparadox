## Action

It just leans and keeps its roots." *Beneath the easy posture, Beta sends a thin tendril of empathic warmth toward the treeline, not to push, only to feel whether the watcher is hunting, hiding, or simply as curious as we are, and murmurs without moving the lips:* "If it's a stranger who's only looking, then it's no different from us on this hill. Let me listen first, and if I say *walk*, we walk, together and at the pace of a man heading home for supper."

Alpha's dread settles into a listening silence, and the body's shared pulse evens out, though the treeline has gone very still.
