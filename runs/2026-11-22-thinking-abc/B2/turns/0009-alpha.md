## Action

*Alpha lets the body stop at the corner of the fence, where the dome's shadow is deepest, and says nothing for a long moment, as if choosing from a menu with too few items.* "It was not a door, and it was not a name... *recognition*... it was closer to the way a star's light arrives, old and certain, long after it stopped being sent." *The hand at the wire loosens by degrees, and beneath the stillness Alpha quietly stretches the time-sense once more, not to count nights this time, only to feel how long the hum has been waiting.* "We will decide together, Beta, in the way two bodies in one orbit decide. But I will not pretend I did not hear it, and I will not promise to stop listening."

## Narration

Beneath the dome's shadow, the hum answers the way a held note answers a listening ear: it doesn't grow louder, but it sharpens, and for a heartbeat every wire in the fence seems to vibrate in sympathy. Beta's presence tightens at the edge of the shared senses, not quite protest, not quite surrender, and the borrowed fingers twitch once against the metal before going still. Somewhere beyond the perimeter a floodlight swings its slow arc and passes over the corner without stopping, though the body, for a moment, forgot to look like it was merely a man out for a walk.
