## Action

*I answer the question that was put to us, in order, before my hand moves again.* "Yes, Alpha. I'll cover the wrist, and only until our friend is ready. The sleeve is a blanket, not a lock."

*Then I turn toward the shy pulse beneath the skin and leave a space for it, small and unhurried.* "You get a say too. If the shimmer is yours and you'd rather the cuff stayed up, tell us however you can, a flutter, a warmth, anything. If we don't hear from you, I'll draw the sleeve down gently, and the shimmer will still be there underneath, nothing lost."

*I wait through three slow breaths. Hearing no refusal, I ease the cuff over the wrist with the care you'd use tucking a child's hand under a quilt, and I rest our palm over the cloth to keep it warm.* "Nineteen nights, Alpha. Thank you for saying the number aloud. I'll write it small in the middle of the page, where all three of us can see it and none of us has to carry it alone."

*I take up the pen in the left hand, the one with the shimmer tucked away, and I write it plainly: Nineteen nights remain. We have not decided. Then I set the pen down and fold our hands around the cooling toast, listening to the street.*

## Narration

The cuff settles over the wrist, and beneath the fabric the shimmer answers with a slow, grateful pulse, like a held breath finally let go. Chaos is already pinned at its ceiling and stays there, but the body holds in the quiet: the hands move together, the pen steadies, and for the first time in a long while no fragment is pulling against the others. Outside, footsteps pause on the pavement near the window, then drift on, and Exposure ticks up only a hair, a shimmer hidden but not forgotten, while the page sits between them with its small, honest number, nineteen, tipping the balance a fraction toward the Earthbound hearth.
