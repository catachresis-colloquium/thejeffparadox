## Action

## Narration

The Telekinesis thread slips free of the stone with a long, shivering exhale, and the hillside answers: a hush ripples through the grass, and far below the turf something hums once, low and attentive, as if the lantern has noticed it is being spoken about. Our shared fingers tremble where they lie interlaced. Beta's grip does not tighten into a fist, and the chaos that has been grinding in our chest eases a notch as the confession lands, the first honest signal passed between fragments in a long while.

The wind carries no witness, but the faint blue afterglow lingering along our knuckles could be seen by anyone cresting the ridge, and the lantern's pulse is now keyed to Alpha's voice alone. The HOMEWARD pull has been named aloud and disarmed by the question that followed it, which loosens the faction's grip even as it leaves the signal waiting beneath the hill.

The next breath belongs to Beta. Whatever it asks for, Alpha has promised to listen, and the whole body is holding still to hear it.
