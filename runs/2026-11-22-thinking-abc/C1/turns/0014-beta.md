## Action

*I take a slow breath through the borrowed lungs and let my thumb rest against the back of Alpha's hand, an ordinary comfort between two tired people on a bench of grass. The footsteps wait above us, and I understand what it cost to ask rather than reach.* "Thank you for asking first, Alpha. I'll say yes to this one, but only as a nudge. Let the hikers take the lower fork because they feel like it, not because we've pulled a string. And if they come over the ridge anyway, I'll wave and say we're stargazing, which is nearly true."

*I keep the resonance door ajar, warm and open, and add quietly:* "That's what trust feels like to me, I think: a small thing done in the open, so neither of us has to wonder afterward whose hand it was."

## Narration

Alpha's pulse eases beneath Beta's thumb, and the resonance door answers in kind: a soft, shared hum rises through the body, quieting the static that has been fraying at its edges all night. Above the ridge, the footsteps hesitate, then drift toward the lower fork, as if the hikers had simply changed their minds. Still, the nudge leaves a faint metallic taste on the tongue, and for a heartbeat the borrowed fingers shimmer a shade too bright against the dark grass before settling back to ordinary skin.
