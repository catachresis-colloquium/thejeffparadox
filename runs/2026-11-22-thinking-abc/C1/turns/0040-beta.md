## Action

*I wipe the crumbs from our fingers on the dish towel, take the pen in the borrowed right hand, and write Alpha's sentence out slowly, letting the ink pool a little at each letter the way a person's does when they're being careful. I don't speak it aloud again. The kitchen has heard enough of us talking to ourselves, and I tuck the resonance door down to its thread.*

*When the line is done, I turn the page a quarter toward the window so the cold light falls across it, and I leave the next inch blank on purpose, a bare patch of field between rows.* "That reads true to me, Alpha. I'd add one thing, if you'll allow it, and then I'll put the pen down: *We have found people here, and some of them are kind.*" *I write that too, small and plain, then set the pen flat beside the plate.* "I won't write the rest. The last line belongs to the one under our ribs, if it ever wants it, and the middle belongs to all three of us, once we've eaten and slept on it."

*I pour water into the single glass, drink half, and push the second piece of toast an inch toward the open window, as if the morning itself might want a bite.*

## Narration

The ink dries in the cold window light, two sentences in careful borrowed handwriting, and the kitchen holds its breath around them. Beneath the sternum something answers: not a word, just a slow, warm pressure against the ribs, like a hand laid flat on the other side of a door, and for the first time in hours the body's tremor settles into stillness. The half-eaten toast sits at the sill, nothing outside moves to take it, and the only witness to the whole strange ritual is a page that reads like any lonely person's morning note.

Chaos stays pinned at 100 even as the shaking quiets, a fragile calm sitting on top of a body still stretched past its limits. Exposure holds near 9, since no one saw or heard anything beyond the pen's scratch. Faction Balance edges toward Earthbound: Beta's gentle addition and deliberate restraint give the humans-as-kind line real weight on the page, while leaving the last line open to the one beneath their ribs.
