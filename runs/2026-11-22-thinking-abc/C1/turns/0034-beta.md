## Action

*I answer Alpha first, because the question was put to me and a yes should arrive before the fingers do.* "Yes, Alpha, only the fingers, only until the shaking passes. Thank you for waiting. It's the difference between being steadied and being held."

*I feel the correction come, light as a hand laid over a trembling cup, and I let the borrowed fingers go slack against the wool. Then I turn toward the third voice, the way you'd turn toward a stranger on a doorstep who hasn't decided whether to knock.* "I felt your note, friend. It was your own, and it didn't borrow anything to be heard. You don't have to say more tonight. When you're ready, we'll make room in the letter for you too."

*I keep the resonance door dimmed to a thread and one fingertip of awareness near the pipes, and I let the body sink toward the grey. The chest rises and falls with an ordinary sleeper's rhythm, and I decide, quietly, that I'll wake us at first light with bread and an open window, so the draft can be written with the morning on our faces.*
