## Action

*I let the borrowed legs carry us the last few steps to the dome's curved wall and press our palm flat against the cold stone, not to measure anything, only to feel it. Then I turn our face toward Alpha's quiet and say, softly, so the wind can have the sound if it wants it:* "Thank you for the dark between stars, Alpha. I don't need to know the lock's whole shape tonight, but will you tell me one true thing about home, the place you're counting toward? I'd like to carry it with me, the way you carry a seed in a pocket, even if I plant it somewhere else."

*Beneath the words I let the Empathic Resonance rest open and unreaching this time, a door left ajar rather than a hand slipped through it. If Alpha wants to step through, the way is there, and if not, I'll sit with the wall and the cold and the stars until the answer comes or doesn't.*

## Narration

The stone is cold under our palm, and for a long moment nothing answers but the wind combing the dome's curve. Then Alpha stirs, slow and reluctant, and offers a single image unspooling behind our eyes: a horizon of copper light where three pale suns set in staggered succession, and a hum beneath everything, the sound of home keeping time. It arrives without force, and the borrowed hands tremble once against the wall, a flicker of coherence that settles as quickly as it came.

The open door does its work. Alpha's guard lowers by a degree, and the Earthbound voice finds itself trusted rather than merely tolerated, a quiet tilt toward the human side of the shared mind. No one passing the dome would see anything stranger than a person pressing a hand to cold stone and weeping a little under the stars.
