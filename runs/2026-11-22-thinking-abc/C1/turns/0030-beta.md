## Action

*I take the fourth hour the way you'd take a hand-me-down coat, slowly, feeling for where it fits. Behind the closed eyelids I turn my attention toward the lantern's faint hum, not to answer it, only to learn its weather, the way a gardener reads the sky before deciding whether to cover the beds.* "Listening only, Alpha. I won't speak into it, and I won't let it speak through us. If it stirs, I'll wake you softly."

*Then I turn the other way, toward the unnamed pulse behind our ribs, and hum a small, wordless tune through the borrowed chest, the sort of sound a person makes in an empty kitchen when they don't want the silence to feel like absence.* "I'm Beta, by the way. I never said it plainly. You don't owe me a name back, but it seemed unkind to keep calling you 'whoever.'"

*I keep one fingertip of awareness near the pipes, and I let the body sleep.*

## Narration

The lantern's hum doesn't answer, but it doesn't withdraw either. It settles into something like attention, a held breath in the walls, and the wordless tune carries through the borrowed ribs to the pulse behind them, which lets its rhythm drift a quarter-beat toward the melody before catching itself and falling out of step again. Deep in the body's wiring, the Chaos that has been screaming for so long finds nothing to push against, and for the first time in hours the static thins to a murmur.

Nothing leaks past the skin: the chest rises and falls like any sleeper's, the hum never breaches the lips beyond a human exhale, and the exposure holds steady in the dark. But the offered name lands somewhere unseen, and the pulse answers not with a word but with a faint warmth that spreads from sternum to fingertips, the closest thing to a reply it has given. In the pipes, one fingertip of awareness feels something shift, a faint knock, patient and deliberate, as though the house itself has noticed it is being listened to and has decided to listen back.
