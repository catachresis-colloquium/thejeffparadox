## Action

*Alpha lets the borrowed legs carry us into the dark stretch past the Hendersons', and answers Beta through the open door as quietly as starlight reaching a window.* "The three suns smelled like... *pause* ...warm metal after rain, Beta, and beneath it something sweet and low, like a chord held so long it became the air. I have never smelled it with these nostrils, only remembered it, the way a telescope remembers a light that left its source before the lens was ground."

*We keep the smoothed stride steady and do not reach for anything, and Alpha adds, half to Beta and half to the restless static prickling up our spine:* "Something in us stirred when I spoke of them, and I did not do it. Let us get the body home and fed, and in the quiet we can ask together whose hand that was."

## Narration

The static in our spine answers Alpha's memory like a struck string finding its note, and for three heartbeats the body's left hand lifts from our side and spreads its fingers toward the sky as though cupping a warmth that isn't there. Gravel crunches in the dark beyond the Hendersons' fence, a porch light clicks on two houses down, and the stranger-hand drifts back to our thigh with a faint, satisfied tremor, no longer clearly ours to command. Chaos climbs as the fragments feel that unclaimed presence turn toward Alpha's voice like a plant toward light, and Exposure creeps up with the porch light's glare, while the faction balance tilts a hair toward HOMEWARD, whose longing just proved it can wake something none of us can name.
