## Action

*I let our shoulders drop the last half-inch and take Alpha's invitation, tipping our head back toward the pale dome the way a person does when they've simply stopped to look at a sky. The stranger's glance is still on us. I meet it with a small, ordinary nod, the nod you give a neighbor on a cold morning, and it slides away.*

"Thank you for saying the hum out loud, Alpha. Naming a thing is like turning the soil over, it stops being a rumor under the ground." *I keep my voice just below the wind, and my hand rests over the coat pocket.* "We'll stay at this bend until your count feels easy, and nobody is racing the hill. Friend, I felt your rustle. Take as long as you like."

*I scan the road ahead with plain eyes, noting the gate, the gravel verge, the long shadow of the dome. Then I turn toward the unnamed pulse and offer the one thing I can without reaching.* "I don't know what the hill means to you. I only know it's easier to look at a hard thing with company. If it helps, I'll describe what I see aloud, in a small voice, and you can tell me if it matches what you feel. The gate is green. The grass beside it is still wet. Nothing up there has moved."

*I keep the sleeve snug, the resonance door dimmed to a thread, and wait, breathing in time with Alpha's count.*

## Narration

The stranger's gaze drifts to the dome and stays there, and the road settles into the plain quiet of three people who simply happen to be standing at a bend. Beta's murmured inventory of the gate and the wet grass lands somewhere beneath hearing, and the unnamed pulse answers it, its rustle loosening into a slow, uneven rhythm that begins to track Alpha's count rather than fight it. Beneath the calm, the body still hums at the edge of failure, its seams holding only because no one is pulling on them.

*Exposure slips to 15 as the stranger's interest dissolves into ordinary indifference. Chaos holds at 100, though the shared breathing gives it no new fuel, and the Earthbound lean tips the faction balance a hair to -5.*
