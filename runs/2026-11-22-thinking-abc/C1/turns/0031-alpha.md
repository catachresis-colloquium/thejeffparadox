## Narration

The pipes settle into a long, creaking silence, and Beta, holding the watch, hears the confession the way a person hears a floorboard give under a familiar tread: not alarm, but the sudden weight of something that was always there. Alpha's slack hands stay perfectly still on the blanket, and that stillness reads as trust, so Beta's answer comes slowly and without the edge the lantern light might have sharpened. The body itself stays quiet, though Chaos remains at its ceiling, a held breath no one dares to exhale.
