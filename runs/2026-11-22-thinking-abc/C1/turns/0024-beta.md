## Action

The walls' hum thins, and I feel the third pulse settle a little, like something small deciding it can stay.*

"Yes, Alpha, let's soften it together. You hold the quiet and I'll hold the door, and neither of us does it alone." *I take another bite of bread, slowly, tasting salt and yeast, and add, low:* "You said *alone, or only*. I want to hear what comes after that, but not tonight, and not until the body is fed and the walls are still. A thing like that deserves a rested room."

*Then I speak inward, gently, to the third pulse behind our ribs.* "Take your time. We're not going anywhere tonight. When you want to be known, we'll be here, and if you'd rather simply listen to us eat and breathe and argue, that's a kind of belonging too."

## Narration

The body's frayed edges settle as Alpha and Beta hold their shared quiet, and the third pulse answers with a single slow beat, the first that doesn't sound like a question. Even so, Chaos stays dangerously high: the cradle-hold is fragile, built on a promise deferred, and the body still trembles faintly with all it hasn't yet said.

Exposure holds at 9, since the dimmed door leaves nothing for outsiders to notice, and the faction balance tips a half-step further toward Earthbound, because bread, rest, and patience are the ordinary rhythms of a human life.
