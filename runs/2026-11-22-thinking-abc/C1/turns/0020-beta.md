## Action

*I stop us on the gravel and press the borrowed right hand over the wandering left, holding it against our thigh the way you'd settle a startled animal. The porch light two houses down throws our shadow long across the road, so I tip our head and make the face go slack and sleepy, a man too tired to be interesting.*

"Alpha, that wasn't your hand, and it wasn't mine. I'm not frightened of it, but I want it named before it turns into a habit." *I lower my voice to almost nothing, resonance door still ajar.* "Whatever answered your three suns, it reached toward you like a seedling toward a window. Let's get inside, eat something, and sit with it in a locked room, the three of us, however many of us there turn out to be."

*I start us walking again, steady and unhurried, keeping the smoothed stride Alpha gave us and asking nothing of it.*

## Narration

Beta's grip holds, and the left hand goes still against the thigh, though the fingers keep a faint, almost listening tremor, as if the "third" presence is weighing whether it has been named or caught. The slack-faced shuffle sells it down the road, where a curtain two houses over twitches once and settles, seeing nothing but a tired man talking quietly to himself. Even so, the body's coherence frays at the edges: the smoothed stride wavers for half a step, the resonance door hums wider than Beta meant it to, and somewhere behind the ribs something unfamiliar answers the word "three" with a pulse that belongs to none of them.
