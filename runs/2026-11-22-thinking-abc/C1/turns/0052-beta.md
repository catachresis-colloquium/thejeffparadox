When Beta lifts a plain, friendly wave, the figure hesitates, then raises a hand halfway, and the thread of resonance at the wrist hums once, curious and unalarmed, as though the door itself had been waved at too. The watcher is now a known quantity, and whether they become neighbor or nuisance rests on the next few seconds.

Exposure creeps up slightly (+2, to 18) since being seen attending to the follower marks the body as aware of surveillance, but the gesture is so ordinary it reads as harmless. Chaos holds steady at its ceiling, the fragments quieter than they've been all walk, and the faction balance tilts a touch further toward the Earthbound (-3) as a human-shaped solution quietly outperforms an alien one.
