## Action

*I lower this body onto the low stone wall and let the cold of it come up through the coat, ordinary and undramatic. For a time I only look at the dome, the way one regards a star from a great distance, and I do not hide that I am listening for the pull. When it comes, it is brief and bright, and I carry it across to Beta as promised.* "It spoke," *I say, and pause, selecting.* "Four words, in the oldest register. *The window is closing.* No more than that. It did not ask me to rise. It only wanted me to know the number is still true."

*I turn the borrowed hand over in Beta's and study the lines of the palm like a chart of a coast I have only heard described.* "I am not going to go in today. I would like to say that aloud so the wall can hear it, and so you can hold me to it." *Then, lightly, with something almost like humor:* "But I would like to learn what the dome looks like from here, from the outside, as you asked. Tell me what you see. Human eyes are supposed to be good at that, the seeing of ordinary things."

*I take the thermos from the stone beside us, pour a measure into the cap, and offer it across, steam rising between our joined hands. I nudge no odds, and I do not count the nights. For this hour I am only a man on a wall, with his companion's shoulder against his own, watching the sun climb a hill that holds something very old beneath it.*
