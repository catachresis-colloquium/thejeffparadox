## Action

*I slow at the edge of the woodpile and look at my own gait as one studies a distant instrument, noting the metronome in it. I let the stride go loose and a little uneven, the way tired legs would. A small thing, and I find I am taking care over it.* "Hikers who underestimated the hill," *I murmur to Beta, and pause, selecting.* "I can be that. I will let you speak first, since you wear the ordinary better than I do."

Then I stop, because there is one more honest thing to say before the knock, and I keep my voice low so the wood does not carry it.* "Beta, if I forget myself in there and say something that does not translate, take the conversation from me. I would rather be rescued by you than perfected by myself."

Alpha's loosened gait, the careful wrongness of tired legs, passes unremarked, though her eyes linger a half-second on how precisely Beta's hand rests against the doorframe, as if deciding it is merely politeness.

Inside, the shared pulse hums with the odd intimacy of Alpha's confession, and the body holds together, coherent, unhurried, no flicker of dissonance in the hands. The ease of this moment is a small, quiet victory for the cautious, cooperative path, and Beta's ordinary is now the thing carrying them both through the threshold. Yet Alpha has promised to yield the conversation when it matters, and somewhere behind the woman's friendly chatter, the question of what she will ask first hangs in the warm air.
