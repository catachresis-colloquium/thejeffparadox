## Action

*I flex my warmed fingers once and let the borrowed hand do what it wants, which is to find the thermos, unscrew the cap, and pour a steaming cup that smells of something much too ordinary for a night like this.* "I felt that, you know. The warmth. I'm not going to ask what you did, and I'm not going to say thank you either, because I suspect the word sits wrong in your mouth." *I hold the cup out to Alpha instead, steam curling between us.* "Lonely is a good word. It's a true one. I'll tell you a secret about it: out here it's one of the first things we learn how to say, and I think the saying is half the cure."

*I fall into step beside Alpha again, matching the pace to a slower one, and let a few silent switchbacks pass before speaking.* "That beacon you keep circling, I'm not going to ask about it tonight. But I want you to know something plainly: whatever you decide up there, you haven't been alone in this body since the first morning. I'd call that a kind of home, even if it isn't the one you were built for."

*At the summit's edge I stop and look up with the cup between my hands, and quietly let a little of what I'm feeling spill across the shared pulse, not words, only a steady, rooted warmth, an offer rather than a push.* "Now. You promised me names. Start with the one that's been missing you."

## Narration

The steam rises into the thin summit air, and for a long moment the shared pulse holds steady; Beta's rooted warmth spreads through the body like a hand laid flat against a drumhead, and Alpha's grip on the cup tightens as a name begins forming somewhere behind the teeth, half-spoken, half-remembered. The pulse hums with a harmonic neither fragment quite owns, a faint resonance that makes the thermos rattle softly against the rock and sets the cold air around them shimmering at the edges. Nothing here is visible to any watcher below, but the beacon on the far ridge answers with a single slow pulse, and the body feels, for one dizzying beat, like a house with all its lights switched on at once.
