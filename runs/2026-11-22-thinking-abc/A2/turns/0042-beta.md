Still, Beta's remark about being "the strangest thing at this table" lingers in the air a beat too long, a half-truth that could be taken for a joke now but might later be recalled as a confession.

Exposure creeps up slightly, Chaos wobbles as the body holds a human warmth that some fragments find intoxicating and others find suffocating, and the faction balance tips another notch toward Earthbound.
