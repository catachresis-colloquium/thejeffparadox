## Action

*I take the cup from Beta's hands and hold it without drinking, letting the steam warm this borrowed face while the beacon's slow pulse fades along the far ridge. The name is still behind my teeth, and I do not speak it yet. I look at it from a distance, as I would a star whose light has taken a very long time to arrive.* "There is a name," *I say, and pause, selecting.* "It does not translate. The nearest shape of it in your language is something like... *the place that keeps a light on for the one who has not yet returned*. I have never said it aloud in this body."

*I drink at last, and the plain heat of it goes down like an ordinary fact. Without turning my head, I note where the sky will open into its alignment, how many nights remain before the window closes, and I set the figure down carefully, like an instrument I am not ready to use.* "Beta, I will not go to the ridge tonight. I want that understood as a choice and not a failure of nerve. The alignment will keep, and I find I would rather spend this one evening being a man on a hill with someone who asked me for a name instead of a signal."

*I lower myself onto the cold rock and gesture the borrowed hand at the space beside me, an invitation clumsy with unfamiliarity.* "Sit. I will give you the lights in order, nearest to farthest, and then, if the body can bear it, we will not name anything at all for a while. I am told that is called company."

## Narration

Beta takes the offered space on the rock without a word, and the body's pulse settles into a slow, even rhythm as the borrowed lungs fill with cold air. It is the first time in many nights that no fragment is pushing against another for the hands. But the phrase "this body" has landed, and Beta's stillness is the careful kind, the stillness of someone who has heard something they will not mention yet. Far along the ridge the beacon has gone dark, and the unspoken figure of the closing window sits in Alpha's chest, patient and unforgotten.
