## Action

*I notice the sugar packet a half-second after the waitress does, and I do not look at it. I rest my palm flat on the Formica and let it sit there heavy and ordinary, a hand that has never moved anything it was not touching. The packet stops. I did not call it back; I only quit reaching. That is the whole lesson of this morning, and I am a slow student.*

"Marrow," *I say, and pause, selecting.* "Thank you for the name. It sounds like something that holds a structure up from the inside." *I glance toward the waitress and offer her a rueful, tired-friend smile, the kind that says caffeine has not yet arrived.* "Long night. My hands are not their best before the second cup."

*Beneath the table I press my knee back against Beta's, and in the quiet of the shared pulse I let the GM's question stand, unanswered but not unheard. What if the other faction is right? I turn it over like a stone from a riverbed. I do not agree. I do not dismiss it either, and the not-dismissing is new.*

*To Marrow, evenly, with both hands visible:* "You said we already know why you came. I would rather you tell us than have us guess, because a guess from me tends to arrive too precisely, as you noticed. Start with the walk. We will listen to all of it, and neither of us will leave the booth until you have finished."

## Narration

The waitress's eyes flick from the stalled sugar packet to Alpha's flat palm, and whatever she half-saw dissolves into a shrug and a refill of Marrow's cup; the moment passes unremarked, though the cooling hum in the shared pulse tells Alpha how close the margin was. Marrow sets down the stirrer and goes very still, the way a person does when an invitation is more honest than they were braced for, and for the first time since sitting down they begin to speak about the walk, haltingly, as if testing whether the booth will hold.

Beneath the table, Beta registers the knee pressed against theirs and the question left standing in the quiet, and the contact lands as a held door rather than a restraint. The body's coherence steadies for a breath, and Alpha's refusal to reach has quietly given the HOMEWARD side a small win in restraint, even as it concedes ground on the argument it declined to dismiss.
