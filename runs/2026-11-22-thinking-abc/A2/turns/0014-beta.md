## Action

*I take the cup in both hands and drink slowly, letting Alpha's confession settle between us the way snow settles on a field, without sound and without hurry.* "Thank you for telling me. I know that wasn't a small thing to set down on a cold rock." *I keep my voice easy, because a hand held too tightly closes whatever it's holding.* "I won't promise tomorrow either, Alpha. But I'll be there when you decide, and I'll tell you what I honestly think, even if it's not what you hope for. That's the only kind of company worth the name."

*I shift the coat higher across our shoulders and lean my head against the cold stone. Under the quiet, I let a thread of empathic resonance hum through the shared pulse, and I don't read anything from it, only listen for whether the wanting-to-wake-it is still burning as hot as the words suggested. It feels less like a fire now and more like an ember someone is tired of carrying.* "Here's what I'd like to do next, if you're willing: finish the thermos, let this body sleep somewhere warm, and see what the morning has to say. Mornings are good at that. They don't solve anything, they just change which questions are loudest."

*I stand, offer a hand, and add with a small crooked smile:* "And Alpha? Antares. I think it chose you back."

## Narration

Alpha's hand closes around Beta's, and the body rises as one for the first time in hours, the thermos tucked under an arm and the borrowed coat trailing behind them like a shared shadow. The ember in the shared pulse doesn't go out, but it dims to something that can be carried, and the faint static that had been crawling behind their eyes settles into a low, even hum. Somewhere above the rooftops, Antares flickers red through a thin seam in the clouds, and for a moment no fragment argues about whether it's watching.

*Chaos eases (-3), Exposure holds steady, and the faction balance shifts a hair toward Earthbound as Beta's patience earns trust that argument never could.*
