## Action

*I finish the coffee slowly, because the body seems to like the way the heat travels, and I let the host's chatter about the radiator and the mittens wash over me like warm water. Nothing decided. Only looking."

## Narration

The porch steps creak under their combined weight, and the host's farewell drifts out behind them, a little too bright, a little too lingering, as if she's filing away the oddness of two hikers who carried a single thermos between three hands. Beta's offer of "the first coffee is on us" lands as pure warmth, but the body flickers with a faint, dissonant hum, the way a tuning fork answers a note it wasn't struck by. Out in the cold daylight, shoulder pressed to Alpha's, the quiet request to simply *look* at the observatory settles between them like a held breath, and somewhere beneath the surface, a more guarded fragment stirs, unconvinced that looking is ever only looking.
