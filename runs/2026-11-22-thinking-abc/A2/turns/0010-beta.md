## Action

*I settle onto the cold rock beside Alpha, close enough that our shoulders share warmth, and for a long minute I say nothing at all. The silence is the gift here, and I let it be one. When I do speak, I keep my eyes on the sky and my voice low.* "You said *this body*. I noticed. I'm not going to make anything of it tonight, except to say that I've been calling it ours, and I'd like to keep doing that, if you'll let me."

*I take the cup back, sip, and pass it again, a small human rhythm I'm still learning to love.* "That name you gave me, the place that keeps a light on. Out here we'd say it's what a home is, and it doesn't need a location, only someone who stays up. I think you've been leaving the porch light on for a place that may not be able to see it. Maybe tonight you let someone leave one on for you."

*I lean my head back against the stone and let the steady rooted warmth hum quietly through the shared pulse again, an offer, not a push.* "Now. Nearest to farthest, you said. I'll try to hold my questions until the end, and when I fail, I hope you'll forgive me. Curiosity is the one habit I picked up before I ever had hands."

## Narration

Alpha is quiet for a long moment, and then something loosens: the body's shared pulse, which has been holding a careful, braced rhythm, eases into Beta's steady one, and the cup trembles slightly as it passes back. "Nearest to farthest," Alpha murmurs, and begins to speak of the closest star, the nearest of its dead worlds, in a low voice that is half confession and half lullaby. *The slip about "this body" has been heard and gently set aside, so no thread is pulled, and no one else is near enough to hear what is being said under the sky.*

The warmth hums on, and the body feels less like a contested territory than a place someone has decided to stay awake in. This is a small, real win for the Earthbound, though it is trust given and not ground taken, and it can only hold as long as it stays that way.
