## Action

*I curl the borrowed fingers closed, slowly, the way you'd cup a seedling against the wind, and let the pulse settle under my palm.* "Navigation, you call it. Funny, I thought sentiment was just roots reaching toward whatever's nearest, and I'm not sure the nearest thing is that hill." *I soften my voice, wanting this body to sound like itself again, and ask:* "Alpha, when you look up at those lights, do you feel pulled, or do you feel *missed*? Because I've been learning the difference lately, and I'd walk out there with you either way, but I'd like to know which of us we're bringing along."

## Narration

The hand stills, and the stray tremor that had been running up the forearm all evening quiets in its wake, as though the body itself were relieved to be spoken to like a person. Alpha doesn't answer right away; the silence stretches long enough that the two fragments feel the chest rise and fall once, twice, in a rhythm that belongs to neither of them. When the voice finally comes, it catches on the word *missed*, as if it had been waiting years for someone to offer the question.

The pull toward the hill hasn't vanished, but it has loosened its grip. Beta's gentleness has bought the Earthbound faction a foothold, and for the first time tonight the body feels less like contested ground and more like shared territory. Nothing about the moment reads as strange to any watcher, since it's only a man standing very still beneath the stars, hand pressed to his own pulse, looking like he's about to cry.
