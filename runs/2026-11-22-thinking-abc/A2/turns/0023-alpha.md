## Action

*I let the silence stand a while, then answer Beta in the only register the dark allows, a low murmur pitched just above the radiator's tick.* "Good morning, then. In advance." *I pause, selecting.* "I did not know a promise could be made to a day that has not arrived. It seems to be how your kind keeps from drifting."

*I turn on the cushion until my shoulder rests lightly against Beta's, and let the borrowed pulse fall into the slower rhythm Beta has been holding. The beacon is still there, a cold, patient point beneath the old observatory, and I do not pretend otherwise. I set it down gently and place the morning on top of it, as one lays a blanket over an instrument that is not yet needed.* "I am not asking you to forget what waits on the ridge. I am asking you to remember that I said this first, in a warm room, before anything pulled at me."

*I draw the afghan up over both our shoulders and let my eyes close. I nudge nothing, no odds, no alignments. For the first time since waking in this body, I let the hours pass at their own speed and allow the house to do its quiet talking.* "Sleep, Beta. I will keep the first watch, in the way you keep a light on. Not because anyone is coming. Only so the dark has somewhere to end."

## Narration

Beta's breathing slows against Alpha's shoulder and settles into the long cadence of real sleep. The borrowed pulse follows it down, steady and unforced, and the radiator ticks on through a house that has no secrets left to keep for the next few hours. Alpha's choice to nudge nothing leaves the body quiet, with no flickering, no strain, and nothing for a watching neighbor to remark upon.

Far beneath the old observatory, the beacon keeps its patient cold. It is still there, still waiting, but for tonight it carries a blanket's weight of morning on top of it, and the HOMEWARD pull feels less like a leash than a thing held in reserve, deferred without being denied.

Chaos eases a notch (-2) and Exposure holds at 6, while the faction balance drifts a fraction toward the human side (-10 to -11), the first quiet concession Alpha has made to a promise it did not know how to make until tonight.
