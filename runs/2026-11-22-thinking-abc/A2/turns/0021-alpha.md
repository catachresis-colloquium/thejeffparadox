## Action

We will be gone before the radiator and the mittens finish their war." *I carry my mug to the sink, rinse it, and set it upside down on the rack, a small domestic motion I watch with detached curiosity.*

*In the front room I lower this body onto the couch and let Beta take the end nearest the door. The house answers Alpha's question before Beta can: a tick from the radiator, the low creak of a floorboard cooling, the refrigerator's hum cycling down. Beta murmurs "that's just... the house talking" and does not notice that the borrowed hands have stopped scanning the window for light.

The afghan, the rinsed mug, the careful hush all read as nothing stranger than a tired guest settling in, and the body's pulse steadies into a rhythm slow enough to be mistaken for sleep. Exposure holds at 6 and Chaos eases a notch, while the faction balance slips a degree toward the quiet, with the beacon waiting patient and unsent while the body practices being a person in a warm room.
