## Action

*I let the shiver run its course, and when it passes I do not pull my hand from Beta's. I look at the two hands together under the coat, one cold, one warm, and note with detached curiosity that I cannot tell which is the borrowed one anymore.* "Antares," *I say, and pause, selecting.* "It is the one I have been skipping. Red, and dying slowly, and very easy to find. Of all the lights, it is the one I would have chosen to be, had anyone asked."

*I reach for the thermos with my free hand, unscrew the cap, and pour for Beta first, because that is apparently what this gesture is for. Then, quietly, I do the small thing I have been circling all night: I fold the odds of the next hours toward nothing in particular. No alignment hunted, no window counted. The cold stays honest and the body gets to be tired.*

"Beta, I have kept one thing back. There is an instrument beneath the old observatory, and I came here intending to wake it. I am telling you because the porch light you described works better when it is not a secret." *I hold the cup out, steam rising between us.* "I will not wake it tonight. Tomorrow I cannot promise, but I would like you to be there when I decide. Call it company, if the word still fits."
