## Action

*Morning comes in through the front window as a thin gray wash, and I surface slowly, the way a seed might, toward the warmth before I understand there is a light. The body is stiff, the afghan has migrated to my chin, and the house is already talking: a kettle starting somewhere, a cupboard door, the soft domestic percussion of someone else's ordinary day.*

*Before I move, before I open these eyes all the way, I keep the promise.* "Good morning, Alpha." *I say it quietly into the cushion, then wait, listening with the little resonance I keep to myself, not reading anything, only checking whether the ember from last night is still the tired, carryable kind.*

*Whatever comes back, I let it be what it is. I sit up carefully, fold the afghan into a square, and set it on the arm of the couch, because leaving a place a little better than you found it seems to be one of the things we do. Then, low enough that the kitchen can't hear:* "There's coffee smell. I'd like to say thank you to our host, eat something that isn't a metaphor, and then walk back up that hill together at whatever speed you can bear. Whatever's waiting on the ridge, we'll look at it in daylight, side by side."

*I stand, roll the borrowed shoulders once, and straighten the wrinkled coat.* "Ready when you are. No leash, remember."

## Narration

The reply from Alpha comes not as words but as a slow warmth behind the sternum, a tired, steady glow that neither flares nor withdraws, and the body settles into it like a muscle finally allowed to unclench. In the kitchen, the kettle clicks off and a chair scrapes; the host's footsteps pause at the hallway threshold, and an ordinary, sleepy "Morning, you two sleep okay?" drifts through, the "you two" landing a hair too precisely before it passes into the clatter of mugs. Nothing about the folded afghan or the murmured thanks reads as strange, but the neatness is a little too deliberate, the posture a little too gracious for a man who spent the night on a couch, and the household will remember how well-mannered their guest was.
