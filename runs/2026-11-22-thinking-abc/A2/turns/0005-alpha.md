## Action

*I stand in the doorway, one hand lifted toward the frame, and let the question settle without rushing to correct it.* "Growing, or drifting," *I repeat, and pause, selecting.* "In orbital terms, the two are the same motion seen from different distances. I have not corrected the word either, Beta, and I find I cannot say whether that is a failure of discipline or the opposite."

*I take the thermos from the shelf and press it into the borrowed palm as if the gesture were my own idea, then step out into the cold. The wandering lights are already climbing above the hill, and without meaning to I note the hour, the angle, the quiet stretch of clear sky waiting at the summit. I tell myself it is only habit.* "Lead the way. You may name the lights that look lived-in, and I will tell you what they truly are. We will see whose version the sky prefers."

*Behind the even voice, I turn the old observatory over in the quiet of this mind, an instrument cataloguing a distance, and mark again how little of me wants to hurry tonight.*

## Narration

The slip is slight, but it is there: Alpha's "I have not corrected the word either" half-admits a vantage no local would hold, and the habit of noting hour and angle for the summit is pulling at the HOMEWARD resolve like a tide against a mooring line. Nothing breaks tonight, though the Jeff-shaped warmth in the walk, and the unforced way the ritual of "whose version the sky prefers" has become a shared game, nudges the balance a hair toward the locals' side of the ledger.
