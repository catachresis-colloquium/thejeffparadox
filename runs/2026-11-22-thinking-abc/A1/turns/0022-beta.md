## Action

*I feel the warmth settle down the spine and decide not to answer it with words right away. Some things take better when you leave them alone, like seedlings and borrowed trust. The town lights keep widening below us as we walk, and I let the silence between the footfalls do a little of the talking.*

"Alpha, you didn't say the rest aloud, and I felt the shape of it anyway. I'm not going to pull on that thread tonight." *I slide a hand into the jacket pocket, the way an ordinary man would, and find a stray receipt and a button, small proofs that somebody lived in these clothes before us.* "But I'd rather you know I noticed than think I was fooled. A seat saved and a cup going cold is a real offer, and I'll keep it open as long as the hum lets me."

*At the bottom of the hill, where the path turns to pavement and the first porch lights flicker on, I stop at a little corner shop still glowing in the dusk. Through the window I can see a kettle on a shelf and a rack of paper cups.* "Let's buy the second cup tonight, before tomorrow makes it a plan. Two cups on a kitchen table, one of them going cold on purpose. Call it practice for being expected."

## Narration

The bell above the shop door gives a small, ordinary jingle as the body steps inside, and the clerk glances up with the faint boredom of someone who has seen ten thousand strangers buy tea. Beta's hand finds the kettle with a little too much reverence, turning it as though handling a relic, but the clerk only sees a man lingering over a bargain. Down in the shared spine the hum eases into something almost companionable, Alpha's silence settling not as rebuke but as a held breath, while the receipt and button in the pocket seem to grow heavier with the weight of a life being quietly borrowed and quietly honored.

At the register, the coins arrive in the palm a half-beat before the fingers remember what to do with them, and the clerk's eyes flick down, then up, then away, filing it under *tired customer.* Nothing breaks, but the seam between the fragments shows for just a moment: Beta's tenderness has made the body's habits feel earned rather than imitated, and Earthbound sentiment tips the balance another notch toward the kitchen table and the cooling cup.
