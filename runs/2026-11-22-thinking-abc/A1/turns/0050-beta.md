Inside, Alpha's inward tension eases from a pulled wire to a shared, steadied thing, and the body's weight settles evenly onto both feet.

The doorframe's grain under Beta's palm is ordinary wood, so the agreement made at the threshold is the only thing that hums, a small pact that favors Earthbound caution without silencing Alpha's instincts. Chaos dips a fraction as the fragments synchronize their next move, and Exposure stays flat, since no one has seen anything stranger than a polite guest with mud on his boots.

Somewhere above them, the dome's great shutter ticks in the dark, as if the stone, or whatever answers in this place, has noticed the care being taken and is deciding whether to speak.
