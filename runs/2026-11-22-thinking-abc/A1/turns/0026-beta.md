## Narration

The warmth travels down the shared arm and finds no resistance. Alpha, who has spent the evening braced for a fist, feels something in the neural weave loosen by a single knot, and the beacon at the far end of the bench pulses once, slow and uncommitted, a seed deciding not to decide yet. The body rises, knees creaking their honest complaint, and for a few steps it moves as one organism rather than two tenants arguing over the same coat.

Out on the pavement the streetlights hum their kindness, and the moth, left behind on the lamp's glass, goes on trembling in its borrowed heat. A woman walking a dog glances at the old man holding out an empty hand to no one, then at the faint steadiness in his gait, and decides it is only tenderness, the ordinary kind that old men carry. Nothing about the moment reads as strange to her, and nothing is.
