## Action

*I tilt this borrowed head back and regard the ceiling as though it were sky, the way a drowning thing regards the surface.* "We have been counting, you and I, the revolutions of this temporary world... and the arithmetic has begun to feel like orbits decaying. Tonight the old observatory sits on the edge of my attention, a small gravitational tug I cannot quite name." *I flex the fingers once, studying the sensation of bones and tendons with detached curiosity, then say lightly, as if remarking on the weather:* "Let us walk toward the high ground this evening. I would like to look at the stars from a place that was built for looking, while the sky is still... what is the word... *patient*."

## Narration

The body answers before the committee can weigh in: the knees unlock, the stride lengthens, and Jeff's ordinary feet start carrying all of them uphill along the gravel path, as though the observatory had been written into the muscle memory all along. Somewhere behind the eyes a dissenting fragment grumbles that "the arithmetic of decaying orbits" is not how a person talks, and the phrasing lingers a beat too long in the cool air. A jogger passing the other way glances at the upturned face and the studied, almost anatomical flexing of the fingers, then looks quickly away, filing it under *odd* rather than *alarming*.

*Chaos +3 (the body strains between competing intentions but follows the strongest pull). Exposure +4 (stilted speech and unnatural self-observation noticed by a stranger). Faction Balance shifts +5 toward HOMEWARD as the high ground and the stars gather momentum.*
