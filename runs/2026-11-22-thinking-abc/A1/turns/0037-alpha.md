## Action

*Sleep takes the body in stages, the way a tide takes a shoreline, and I let it. I do not stand watch over the dark. I do not stretch one fiber of attention toward the back wall, and I do not ease the odds by a hair. For one night, the temporary world may keep its own accounts.*

*But before the last edge of thought goes under, I turn toward the one sleeping beside me in the same ribs, and I speak in the quiet inner voice, slowly, choosing each word from the shelf.*

Beta, the count is nine. I said I would tell you every night, and I am telling you. *I pause before the next word, as if selecting it from a menu.* ...Eight, after tomorrow's dawn. I do not know yet whether the number shrinks toward an ending or simply toward a morning. I only know that tonight I am not carrying it alone, and that is a strange new weight, lighter than the old one.

*The key on the nightstand holds its stripe of streetlight. The glass of water keeps its small, unspilled tremor. I let my breathing fall in with the borrowed pulse until I cannot tell which of us is keeping the rhythm.*

If the hum knocks before morning, wake me first. I would rather answer plainly than be found hiding. *Then, last, a thread of warmth along the nerves, my own again, still unpracticed, still crooked, and I do not mark the debt.* Goodnight, Beta. Keep the light on.

## Narration

The body drops into a sleep so even it seems rehearsed, and for once nothing shivers at the back wall; the countdown settles into the dark like a stone into still water, shared instead of hoarded. Somewhere below waking, Beta stirs and answers in a half-formed murmur that never reaches the lips, and the borrowed pulse steadies around it. The key keeps its stripe of streetlight, the glass its unspilled tremor, and the one honest thing Alpha did tonight, trading a secret for trust, tilts the quiet balance a few degrees away from the homeward pull and toward something the two of them have not yet named.

*Chaos holds at 14, the body's coherence easing with the shared rhythm. Exposure stays at 5, since nothing in the sleeping room has changed for any outside eye to catch. Faction Balance shifts to -12, Alpha's open confidence loosening the hard line between the factions.*
