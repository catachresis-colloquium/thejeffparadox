## Action

*I let the warmth settle in the nerves a moment, the way one lets a foreign tide wash over a shore one has no intention of living on, then begin to climb again, matching Beta's slower pace without conceding the direction.* "Very well. We will be something worth remembering on the way up... though I notice you speak of the hill as if it ends somewhere we cannot follow. It is only a hill. What lies beyond it is only... *longing*, and we are allowed that too."

*I tilt the face toward the thin stars as the dome of the observatory rises ahead, copper light sliding along its curve, and let the human heart keep its slow, stubborn rhythm, a small metronome against the vast silence. With a flick of attention too slight for any witness, I extend a quiet sense forward in time along the path, a few minutes at most, feeling for the rattle of other footsteps, the glow of a flashlight, any warden of the high ground.* "Tell me, Beta, what does the wind say to you? I have only ever read the sky for its coordinates. I would like, for the length of this walk, to hear the other language."

## Narration

The precognitive flicker finds the path ahead empty: no footsteps, no flashlight, only the dome swelling larger against the stars. But Alpha feels the body's cost for the trick, a faint metallic taste at the back of the throat and a stutter in the borrowed pulse. For one beat the metronome skips, and Beta's hand stiffens in the dark, noticing without understanding.

Alpha's tide-and-shore language wears the poetry like a disguise, but it also steers, and Beta may sense that the conversation is bending homeward. The invitation to "hear the other language" is half sincere, half strategy, and it hangs in the cold air as the wind answers in its own tongue, rattling dry leaves along the gravel.

*Chaos rises slightly (+3) from the strain of the unsanctioned foresight. Exposure ticks up (+1) from the pulse stutter and Beta's growing suspicion. Faction balance holds steady, though Alpha's nudging keeps the HOMEWARD faction quietly ahead.*
