## Action

*I surface a little from the edge of sleep, the way a swimmer lets the tide hold them, and I do not open the eyes. The ceiling can keep its dim chart. I listen through the borrowed ears to the hum behind the back wall, that shifted pitch, a head tilted toward a plain voice, and I resist the old reflex to measure it. I only note it, and let the note sit in the chest like a stone warming in the sun.*

*Inwardly, soft as a seed pressed into soil:* Alpha, I felt the pitch change too. Whatever's listening heard a man say goodnight and mean it. I'm not going to chase it at this hour. Hospitality has a bedtime, and so do guests.

*I settle the shared hands more loosely against the blanket and let the pulse find its slow tide.* Thank you for the warmth, the unpracticed kind. It's like a first sprout, crooked and entirely real. I'm keeping the porch light on, and I'm keeping the count of nine beside you, out loud in the head, every night. If the hum knocks before morning, wake me, and we'll answer it together, barefoot and honest.

*Then I let my own attention soften, one strand at a time, until all that remains awake is a small, stubborn gladness.* Goodnight, Alpha. Tomorrow there's coffee, and a sky to be held to.

## Narration

Beta's mind goes quiet by degrees, and the shared body follows: the pulse slows, the borrowed hands go slack against the blanket, and the tension that has clung to the jaw all evening finally lets go. Behind the back wall, the hum holds its new pitch for one long moment, then drifts back toward its old register, as if whatever listens has decided that a sleeping man is not worth the effort. In the stillness, Alpha's ritual count of nine settles into the dark like a lullaby, and the fragments rest closer to one body than they have in days.

*Chaos holds steady at 15 and Exposure at 5. The calm costs nothing and gives away nothing, but it counts for Earthbound, tilting the Faction Balance a notch further toward -15.*
