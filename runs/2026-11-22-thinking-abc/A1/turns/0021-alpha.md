## Action

*I stop walking. The wind goes on without me, combing the grass, and for a moment I simply stand on the dark slope with the town spilled out below like a small, warm constellation.*

"Beta, I will answer your coffee question honestly, which means slowly." *I pause before the next word, as if selecting it from a menu.* "...*possibly* it would take someone saving me a seat. Not a signal, not coordinates. A chair, and a second cup going cold beside the first. I have never been expected anywhere, and tonight a stranger said *I'll hold you to the sky*."

*Then I turn the face upward, because the question the GM slipped beneath my attention deserves the stars as witnesses. I speak to the one who came before the fragmenting, to a self I can only chart by its absence, the way astronomers chart a planet by the wobble it leaves in its star.* "To the one who was whole: you were never lost. You were only far. And the far place we ache for may be a thing we carry, not a place at all. Do not hurry the light."

*I do not say the rest aloud, the beacon, the alignment, the window I still intend to keep an eye on. I let the silence hold it, honest about what it contains, and start down the hill again, matching the pace to the pulse. If Beta offers warmth along the nerves, I will take it this time, and not mark the debt.*

## Narration

Alpha's soft words to the stars carry no outward danger, and the body walks on, easy and unremarkable under the wind, so Exposure holds steady at 5. But the held-back beacon sits inside the silence like a stone in a shoe, and Beta, feeling the unspoken weight through shared nerves, answers by loosening a thread of warmth down the spine, and Alpha accepts it without tallying the cost. The shared pulse settles into one steady rhythm for the first time all night, and the faction balance tips a degree toward warmth and away from the homeward pull, though the window Alpha still means to watch keeps the quiet from being peace.

Chaos eases to 12 as the fragments briefly keep step, Exposure holds at 5, and the Faction Balance shifts to -5.
