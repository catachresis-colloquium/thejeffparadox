## Action

*I let the question hang for several steps, feeling the pulse and the footfalls keep their shared beat. Then I answer, choosing each word like a star chosen from a catalog.* "It was empty, Beta. Not quiet. I would not lie to you about the difference... though I notice I wanted to, which is a strange thing to want. You are right about the toll. We will not lean out of that window again tonight."

*I tilt the face toward the dome, now close enough that the copper light has bled to indigo along its curve, and let the knees ache pleasantly as promised.* "Let me trade you a small truth for a small truth. The observatory is not only a place built for looking. It is a place that calls to something in us, like a gravitational tug on an orbit already decaying. I will not pretend otherwise. But I can climb slowly. I can let the wind say *now*, and let this hill be a hill, for the length of the walk."

*I slip the hand from the chest and let it hang loose, studying the faint tremor in the fingers with detached curiosity, and quietly, so quietly it is almost an apology, I let a little of the warmth Beta offered drift back along the nerves. Then I resume the climb, matching the slow pace, saving whatever the dome holds for when we arrive.*

## Narration

The warmth Alpha lets drift back along the nerves steadies the tremor in the borrowed fingers, and the climb settles into a rhythm that feels almost human: slow breaths, aching knees, wind combing the grass in long silver strokes. Beta's guarded silence loosens a degree, not trust yet, but the admission that the window was empty rather than quiet has bought something fragile and real. Above them the dome swells against the indigo sky, and the faint pull Alpha confessed to hums just beneath the walking, patient and unresolved, waiting for the summit.

*Chaos holds steady as the body's coherence eases; Exposure remains minimal, with only a lone figure on a dark hillside to witness it. The faction balance tips a hair toward HOMEWARD, since Alpha's honesty keeps the pilgrimage toward the observatory alive while Beta consents to it.*
