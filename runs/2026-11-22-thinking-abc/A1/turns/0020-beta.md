## Action

*I let the downhill pace slow even further, until the knees are simply enjoying the slope, and I tilt the face up at the stars now spilling thick across the dark. Salt on velvet, just as she said. The borrowed lungs take the cold in long drafts, and for once nobody in the committee is arguing.*

"Alpha, I want to say something plainly, because I think you've earned plainness tonight." *I stop at the bend where the town lights come into view, small and warm in the valley, and press a palm briefly to the chest, feeling the shared heartbeat settle.* "You held the hum without pulling it. You asked instead of reaching. I know what that cost, and I won't pretend it was nothing. It felt like the first time we've both been in the same room at once."

*I start walking again, matching the pace to the pulse.* "I wrote a name in that book, and it felt like putting a hand flat on the ground and feeling the ground push back, gently. Tomorrow there will be coffee and a patient sky. I'd like to bring a second cup, even if only one of us can drink it." *Quietly, I send a thread of warmth along the nerves, steadier now, a habit more than an offer.* "What would it take, do you think, for you to want to come back for the coffee as much as for the hum?"

## Narration

The question hangs in the cold air, and for a long moment the committee stays quiet. Then the body answers before any voice does: the hum swells low in the sternum, unforced, and the town lights below seem to blur and sharpen in time with it. It is not quite yes, but it is the first answer Alpha has given that wasn't a reach or a retreat, and the borrowed hands, loose at the sides, don't curl into fists.

Nothing about the moment is visible from outside, since the only witnesses are the stars and a body walking home slowly under them, so Exposure holds steady and Chaos dips as the shared heartbeat settles into a single rhythm. The Earthbound thread tightens a notch, with plain honesty winning over strategy, and somewhere in the back of the skull a quieter faction stirs, uncertain whether it has just been courted or simply seen.
