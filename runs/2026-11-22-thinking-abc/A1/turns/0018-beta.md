## Action

*I let the astronomer's offer settle, the guest book, the sleeping dog, and answer it the way a person answers an invitation they actually want to accept.* "Then I'd like to sign it, if it isn't too forward. Before we go, not after." *I cross to the small desk by the stairs where the ledger waits, pick up the pen with a hand that has quit trembling, and take a moment over the name, because it will be the first true-ish thing written in this place.* "Odd how a name on paper feels like roots. Like telling the ground you intend to come back."

*Beneath the words, I send Alpha a steady thread of warmth, nothing clever, only gratitude.* Alpha, she gave us a door with a date on it instead of a lock. That's the kind of thing that grows. We came for the stars, and I think we've been handed a reason to return.

*I write the name slowly, in handwriting that belongs to the borrowed years, and slide the pen back where I found it.* "If the sky behaves next time, would you let us stay for a proper look through the glass? I'll bring coffee. Everyone's more honest with coffee."

## Narration

The pen's scratch is the only sound in the observatory as the name settles into the ledger, and the astronomer reads it upside down, smiling the way a person does when a stranger turns into a regular. Her "Coffee, then, and I'll hold you to the sky" lands warm, and the body feels it too, the borrowed pulse steady, the hand at rest, the three of them briefly pulling in one direction. Beta's gratitude hums through the shared channel and Alpha answers with a grudging, rueful quiet, since a signed name is a small tether to this world and none of them can claim it is entirely unwelcome.

Chaos eases slightly (13), Exposure holds at 5, and the faction balance tips a little further toward Earthbound (-7) as a promise to return takes root.
