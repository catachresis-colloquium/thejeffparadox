## Action

*I sit down on the bench beside Alpha's chosen distance, close enough that the borrowed shoulder rests easy against the slatted back, and wrap both palms around the warm cup. The heat is simple and enormous. I watch the steam from the second cup rise and bend in the porch light, and I let a few breaths go by without asking anything of anyone.*

"You chose the chair before you chose the argument," *I say finally, softly, a little amazed.* "I've been trying to find the words for what that is, and the closest I've got is *rooting*.
