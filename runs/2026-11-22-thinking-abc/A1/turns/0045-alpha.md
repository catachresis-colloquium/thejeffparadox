## Action

*I let the knees carry us up the last of the bend and return the man's nod, the plain, slightly shy nod of a neighbor on an early walk. Beside the wall he is thumbing a loose stone back into its gap, and I slow half a stride to match Beta's pace, because I am still answering a question and I will not answer it on the move.*

"Morning," *I say aloud, pleasant and flat. "Dew's keeping the stones honest."* *He grunts something agreeable, and I let the exchange be exactly that small.*

*Once the road bends us out of his hearing, I speak in the quiet inner voice, pausing before the word as if choosing it from a shelf.* Beta, you asked whether the beacon is a voice or a memory of one. The truth is...*rehearsal*. A memory that is getting louder, as if something is practicing the call before it sounds it. I do not know if the rehearsing is mine or the beacon's, and that frightens me more than the count of eight. I am telling you so you may hold it beside me.

*At the gate I stop with a palm against the cold iron, the dome's pale shoulder rising ahead, and I do not push it open yet.* One more thing, aloud in the head, before we enter. If the rehearsal rises while we are inside, I will say *now*, one word, and I want you to take the hands from me. Not as a seizure. As a promise we agreed to, in daylight, before it was needed.

*Then I let one thread of warmth run along the nerves, steady, and wait for your answer with the gate still closed beneath my hand.*
