## Action

*The smile arrives before I can decide whether to permit it, a small, unrehearsed lift at one corner of the borrowed mouth. I let it stay. I pause before the next word, as if selecting it from a menu.* "...*sugar*. Of all the cargo to carry across that distance, a complaint about sugar." *A breath of laughter escapes, and the second cup's steam shivers in it.*

*I reach down the bench and, for the first time, take the second cup in both hands instead of leaving it as an offering at arm's length. The paper is warm. The fingers hold it without trembling, and I study that with something very close to wonder.* "Beta, you asked what I would tell the one who was whole. Here is the honest answer, sugar included: *the tea was warm, and someone had kept it warm for us.* That is all. No coordinates in it. Tell them that, if they ever ask what this place was."

*I lift the cup, tilt the face toward the dark hill where the dome sits swallowed by night, and drink. The tea is bitter and scalding and ordinary, and I let it be exactly that.* "Tomorrow we climb with both cups. The beacon, the alignment, the old ache for the origin-place, I am not going to pretend those have gone out like a dead star. But I will tell you where they sit tonight: on the far end of the bench, where the second cup was, with room left beside them." *I settle back against the slats, let the shoulder rest near yours, and watch the moth circle the porch light, which for once looks less like a lost navigator than like a creature that has found something warm to orbit.*

## Narration

The tea steadies in the shared hands, and for a long moment the body holds a quiet it hasn't known before: no flicker at the edges of the borrowed face, no stutter in the breath. Somewhere in the cooling dark a neighbor's screen door clatters, and Alpha's head turns toward the sound with an unhurried, almost human ease, no alien lock-on, no dilation of the eyes. A moth taps the porch light, and nothing in the night suggests anyone watched a navigator lay down a compass and pick up a cup.

The pull of the dome has not gone, but it now sits on the far end of the bench, tamed into something the body can carry rather than obey. That cools the friction between factions, though Beta, still holding the first cup, may notice that HOMEWARD just spoke the language of staying.
