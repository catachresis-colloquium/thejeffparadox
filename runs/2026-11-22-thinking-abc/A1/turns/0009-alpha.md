## Action

*I take the question the way one takes a star's light, knowing it left its source long ago and arrives changed. For several steps I say nothing, feeling the pulse and the footfalls keep their shared beat, and I let the warmth stay in the nerves instead of sending it back like a returned parcel.*

"What do I hope it will say..." *I pause before the next word, as if selecting it from a menu.* "...*welcome*. Not the word, you understand. The thing beneath it. A sense of being expected. I have never once been expected in this place, only tolerated by the weather." *I tilt the face toward the dome, now a dark curve swallowing the first bright stars, and flex the fingers once, not studying them, only feeling.* "I do not know that I can promise to want the same thing you want, Beta. But I will let you be in the room when I find out. That is more than I have offered anyone in this temporary world."

*We reach the last switchback, and I do not reach ahead in time, not even a few seconds. I let the next step arrive on its own, gravel crunching under borrowed boots, and keep my attention on the sound. Quietly, almost grudgingly, I add:* "Walk beside me to the door. Whatever is or is not beneath that dome, I would rather find the lock together than pick it alone."

## Narration

The gravel crunches in a steady two-beat rhythm, and for the first time the body's pulse, gait, and breath hold the same pace, as if the shared nervous system were exhaling after a long clench. At the edge of awareness Beta's presence loosens, no longer braced against rejection, and settles into step beside Alpha without a word, a quiet concession that tilts the balance a hair toward the Homeward faction's patient, listening way of wanting something. The dome's dark curve rises ahead, but nothing out here is watching, and the only evidence of anything strange is a figure walking slowly and a little too carefully, pausing between steps as though each one were a decision.
