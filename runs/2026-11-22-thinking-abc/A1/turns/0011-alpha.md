## Action

*I do not step across the threshold at once. I stand in the doorway with the cold, brass-scented breath of the place washing over the face, and I listen, the way one listens for the faint hum of a star one has only ever charted. The mechanism above ticked once. I count the silence after it, and I do not like the shape of the number.*

"Slowly, as we agreed," *I murmur, and my hand finds the doorframe, not for balance but because Beta's warmth has taught me that anchoring is a thing bodies do.* "Something above us moved, Beta. Perhaps only old gears settling in the cold. Perhaps not." *I keep my voice at the pitch of a man talking to himself, and I let the head tilt toward the dark of the dome without letting the eyes linger anywhere that would look like seeking.*

*Beneath my attention, a colder thought: if anyone were watching, they would see a man pause too long at a door, speak too carefully, and flex fingers that are not quite his own. So I make the next movement deliberately ordinary. I step inside, scuff my boots on the mat as a person would, and say aloud, lightly, as one says it to an empty room:* "Hello? The door was open." *I use no foresight, no nudge of the odds. Tonight I will let the temporary world be exactly as unhelpful as it chooses.*

## Narration

Your greeting dies in the cold air, and for a long moment the observatory offers nothing back: no answer, no footfall, only the slow tick of the mechanism overhead, which does not repeat. Then, high in the dark of the dome, something shifts with the soft, deliberate sound of weight settling, and a thin line of lamplight slides across the floor from an upper catwalk, as if someone had just turned toward you, or away.

The performance of ordinariness holds; the scuffed boots and the lazy hello are exactly what a harmless visitor would do, and whoever is up there has no reason yet to think otherwise. But Beta's warmth lingers where Alpha's fingers rest on the doorframe, and the borrowed knuckles ache faintly from gripping wood too carefully, a small strain in the body's coherence that Alpha will have to keep managing.
