## Action

*I let the last few steps pass in silence, listening to the gravel and the shared heartbeat, and then, where the path ends at the observatory's iron-banded door, I stop and rest one palm against the cold brick of the wall. It is rough and slightly damp under the fingertips, and I take a moment simply to feel that, because Alpha has just offered something I want to honor properly.*

"Thank you for asking me to the door, Alpha. I know what it cost to say *together* out loud." *I try the handle with the other hand, gently, the way you test whether a seedling will come loose from its pot. If it is locked, I will not force it, and I will not let either of us reach for anything stranger than a knock or a patient wait.* "If it opens, we go in slowly. If it doesn't, we sit on that bench by the wall and let the stars do the work, and I'll tell you the names I've learned for them from this body's old memories, the ones that came with the borrowed years."

The two of you stand on the threshold, one body leaning gently into the quiet, with nothing yet strange enough to be seen.
