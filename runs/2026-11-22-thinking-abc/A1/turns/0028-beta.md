## Action

*I let Alpha's question about the bench settle for a few steps before I answer, because a promise like that deserves to be received properly and not just acknowledged. The streetlights stretch our shadow long and then short again as we pass under each one, and I match my stride to the pulse.*

"That serves, Alpha. More than serves." *I close the borrowed fingers around the hand I'm holding, a gentle pressure, the kind you give a friend crossing a patch of ice.* "A held door is a better thing than a held breath. You don't have to walk through it tonight, and I won't ask you to. I'll just keep leaving the porch light on."

*At the next corner I slow, tilting my face up toward the dark shoulder of the hill where the dome sits. I think of the astronomer's dry voice, the creaking third step, the guest book with its freshly rooted name.* "One request, and it's a small one. Whenever the beacon counts something, tell me the number. Even if it's a number I won't like. I'd rather carry a hard truth beside you than a soft silence behind you. That's how things grow here, I think. Slowly, and in the open."

*Then I turn us toward the lit windows of the town and let a last thread of warmth run along the nerves, steady as a habit now.* "Come on. Let's go find out what this body keeps in its kitchen. I suspect there's a kettle, and I'd like us to learn together what the second cup tastes like when it doesn't have to be a symbol."

## Narration

The warmth Beta threads along the nerves settles into the borrowed hands like a held note, and the body's gait steadies for the first time in blocks, the tremor from the hill's faint hum fading to a murmur. Under the streetlights the two of them look like nothing stranger than a person walking slowly home, though a woman watering her porch ferns pauses mid-pour to watch the figure talk quietly to no one, nodding at answers only it can hear. Behind them the dome keeps its silence for now, and Alpha's quiet reads less like retreat than like a door left ajar, tipping the balance a little further toward Earth.

Chaos eases to 12. Exposure ticks up to 6 from the ferns-watcher's curious glance. Faction Balance shifts to -14.
