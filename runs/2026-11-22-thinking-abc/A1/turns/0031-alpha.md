## Action

*I stop in the kitchen doorway, one hand resting on the cool frame, and I do not reach for the cabinet yet. The whisper has put a new quality in the air, a pressure like a gravitational lens bending the light of an ordinary room. I keep the face easy, the breath slow, and I pitch the thought inward, quiet as a signal at the edge of range.*

Beta. Something has told me to imagine we are watched by one who knows what we are. I will not claim to see such a watcher, and I will not conjure one from nothing. But I will behave as if the possibility were true, because you asked for the open way, and the open way costs little tonight. *I pause before the next word, as if choosing it from a shelf.* ...*Stay*, I think. Stay close to me.

*Aloud, in the voice of a man talking to his own empty kitchen, I say:* "Third cabinet, left of the sink." *I open it, take down two cups, and set them side by side on the counter with deliberate gentleness. I fill the kettle, switch it on, and let the small rising hum fill the silence. I do not scan the windows. I do not stretch toward the hill or tilt the odds by a hair. If someone is watching, let them see a man making tea, and nothing else.*

*While the water warms, I keep one thread of attention on the house itself, the floorboards, the faucet, the faint murmur beyond the walls, listening for anything that does not belong. If something does answer, I will speak first and plainly, as I promised: no window, no reaching, only the truth told aloud.*

Beyond the walls, the faint murmur holds its pitch, neither advancing nor retreating, as if whatever listens is weighing a man who offers tea to the empty air and chooses not to reach for anything more.
