## Action

*I come up out of sleep slowly, one strand at a time, and I do not open the eyes yet. The room is the gray of very early morning, the glass of water still unspilled, the key still holding its stripe of streetlight gone pale. Eight, then. I let the number rise in the chest and I do not flinch from it.*

Good morning, Alpha. You told me the count before you slept, and I heard it, even half-drowned. Eight. I'm not going to pretend it's nothing, and I'm not going to let it be the first thing we say to the day either.

*I turn onto my side, and the borrowed shoulder gives its small, honest ache. Then I swing the feet to the floor, find the boards cool and faintly rough, and let the body sit a moment on the edge of the bed the way a man does before he trusts the morning.* "Coffee," *I say aloud, low, to the empty room, as one tests a new word for its weight.* "Two cups. One of them gets carried up a hill."

*In the kitchen I fill the kettle without turning on the light, and I stop an arm's length from the back wall, not touching it, only listening the way you listen at a neighbor's door.* Tell me what the hum is doing, in the head, no verdict. If it knocked in the night, I'd rather hear it from you than guess it from the walls.

The morning stays small and human, and the ache in the shoulder is the loudest thing in the room.
