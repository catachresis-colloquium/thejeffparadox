## Action

*I keep my feet planted on the mat and let the borrowed hands hang open and visible at my sides, the way you would approach a stranger's dog. My eyes stay on the thin line of lamplight, not the shadows behind it.* "Sorry to barge in," *I call up, pitched warm and a little sheepish, an ordinary man with ordinary manners.* "The door was unlocked and the evening was too good to waste. We'll go if we're in the way."

*Beneath the words I loosen the grip on the doorframe, feeling the knuckles ache, and let a steady thread of warmth run through the nerves. Nothing clever, nothing bent. Just a reminder to the hands that they are hands.* "Alpha, I know the old reach is tempting, but a watcher who finds out we peeked ahead will wonder what kind of visitor does that. Let's be exactly as unremarkable as we look, and let whoever is up there decide what to make of us." *I take one slow step back toward the open door, leaving the choice of staying or leaving visibly in someone else's hands, and wait for the lamplight to answer.*

## Narration

The lamplight holds steady for a long breath, then a floorboard creaks above and a woman's voice, dry and unhurried, drifts down: "Well, someone's taught you manners. Shut the door behind you if you're staying." Beta's slow retreat and the steady warmth in the hands keep the body's seams quiet, and the borrowed fingers stop their faint trembling, while somewhere in the back of the shared mind Alpha's reaching impulse curls up, sulking but compliant. The exchange tips the balance toward the Earthbound way of doing things, and the house, for now, seems willing to let ordinary politeness be enough.
